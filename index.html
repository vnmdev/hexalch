How it works
  • The ladder is the classic transmutation order: Lead → Tin → Iron → Copper → Quicksilver → Silver → Gold (Saturn, Jupiter, Mars, Venus, Mercury, Moon, Sun). Solve a tier to ascend; boards grow and harden as you climb.
  • The board is a hexagon of cells. Lead counts its surrounding cluster; Tin adds neighbour-only halos; Copper adds full-axis spears; Quicksilver adds one-way flows; Silver adds a reflected cluster across the board's centre.
  • Generation: a random solution is made, mixed exact-count clues are computed, then clues are stripped as long as a deduction-only solver can still rebuild the whole board. Every unlocked clue language is kept visible. The result is a unique, no-guess puzzle.
  • Progress is saved in your browser, so you resume at your current metal. Completed tiers stay unlocked — click one to replay it.
